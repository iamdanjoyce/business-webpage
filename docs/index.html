FAQ

Questions business owners actually ask

I don't know anything about websites — is that a problem?

Not at all — most clients don't, and don't need to. I handle the technical side and explain everything in plain terms. You just need to know your business.

Do I need to buy a domain and hosting myself?

No. I can register your domain (e.g. yourbusiness.co.uk) and set up hosting for you as part of the build, or use ones you already own if you have them.

Can I update the site myself later?

Yes. Every site includes a simple way to update text, photos and opening hours, and I'll walk you through it. If you'd rather not, the Care Plan covers small edits for you.

Do you only work with businesses in Bishop Auckland?

Bishop Auckland and County Durham are the core, but I work with clients across the North East. If you're further afield, get in touch and we'll sort a call.

What if I need changes after the site is live?

Small fixes in the first two weeks after launch are included free. After that, the Care Plan covers ongoing edits, or I'm happy to quote one-off changes.
